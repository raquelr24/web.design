Meet Lea

She is dainty but don't underestimate her. While small, she is the real protector of our home. She loves to show off her toys by strutting around the backyard, or greeting you at the door. If you ever lose sight of her, she is probably hiding somewhere she isn't supposed to be -- like under the bed or sitting at the dining table.

Meet Leo

Despite Leo's big-boy demenor, he truly is a sweetheart. He loves doing tricks for treats -- his favorite is to close the garbage door. He loves to bring you his blanket to cuddle and keep you warm on the cold days. You can always find him napping on the couch or in bed.

My brother and I have always wanted a family dog. In 2023, my parents humored us and allowed us to look for a puppy to adopt. Our mom found two pups from the same litter, one was a boy and the other was a girl. When we couldn't decide which puppy to adopt, our parents suggested we adopt "siblings for the siblings." That night my brother and dad decided to drive four hours to pick them up. On September 30th 2023, Leo and Lea came home and completed our family.
